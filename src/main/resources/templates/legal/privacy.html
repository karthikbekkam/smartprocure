Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

SmartProcure Enterprise is committed to protecting your operational data and privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, and safeguard information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect work email addresses, professional contact names, business organization details, tax IDs, and procurement transaction logs strictly necessary for platform operations.

2. Authentication and Security

User passwords are stored using BCrypt cryptographic hashing algorithms. Session security is maintained via signed JSON Web Tokens (JWT) transmitted securely over HTTPS.

3. Use of Information

Collected data is used solely to provide role-gated access to purchase requisitions, supplier onboarding, inventory visibility, 3-way invoice matching, and system audit logging.

4. Data Retention and Protection

We implement technical and organizational security measures to prevent unauthorized data access, disclosure, modification, or destruction.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Security & Compliance team at privacy@smartprocure.local.
