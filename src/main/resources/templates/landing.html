About The Platform

Built For Modern Enterprise Sourcing

Eliminate procurement bottlenecks, reduce rogue spending, and establish automated compliance across your entire organization.

Centralized Procure-to-Pay Workflow

From purchase requisition creation to manager approval, PO dispatch, goods receipt, and final payment settlement.

Multi-Warehouse Stock Control

Track real-time stock levels, receive automated low-stock alerts, and manage inter-warehouse stock transfers seamlessly.

Automated 3-Way Matching

Reconcile Purchase Orders, Receiving Goods Slips, and Vendor Invoices to eliminate overbilling and duplicate payments.

Role-Based Governance

SmartProcure enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to keep team members focused on their authorized responsibilities:

  • Administrator: Full system governance & audit log oversight.
  • Procurement Manager: Requisition approvals & vendor dispatch.
  • Finance Manager: Invoice approval & payment settlement.
  • Warehouse Manager: Stock level adjustments & receiving.
  • Vendor Portal: Order tracking & electronic invoicing.

Security Architecture

Zero-Trust Enterprise Protection

Built with Spring Security 6, JJWT token architecture, BCrypt password hashing, and HttpOnly cookies to keep financial data secure.

JWT Authentication

Short-lived access tokens combined with secure refresh token evaluation.

Role-Based Access

Granular Method-Level Security `@PreAuthorize` authorization.

BCrypt Encryption

Passwords salted and hashed using BCrypt strength 10 algorithm.

Complete Audit Log

Every approval, payment, and inventory update is recorded with user & timestamp.
